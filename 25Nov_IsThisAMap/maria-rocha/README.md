# 25Nov_IsThisAMap: Aracruz, Espírito Santo

**Mapper:** Maria Luiza Freitas Rocha\
**Data:** Google Earth satellite image (Image © 2026 Airbus; Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO), painted by hand with gouache

## About the map and process

For this challenge, I wanted to explore the intersection between cartography and art by creating a map that could also become a painting. I used RStudio to design a template based on a satellite image of Aracruz, Espírito Santo, Brazil, dividing it into a grid of quadrants. Some sections were intentionally left almost blank, with the satellite imagery faded to serve as a guide for painting. I then printed the template on an A3 watercolor paper sheet and used gouache paint to fill these spaces, translating the land use and land cover patterns into colors and textures. The idea was to move beyond the conventional digital representation of geographic data and experiment with a more manual, artistic way of interpreting and representing the landscape.
