# 17Nov_LightDark: The Westland glow

At night the sky above Westland glows orange from the lamps over tomatoes, peppers and cut flowers in one of the densest greenhouse areas in the world. Here every greenhouse in the municipality of Westland is drawn as a glowing orange shape on black, with the road network as a very faint grey trace.

29% of Westland's area is under glass. The darker orange haze around the greenhouses is greenhouse land (the horticulture plots with their paths, water basins and boiler houses), which covers about half of the municipality.

The dark holes are the villages: Naaldwijk in the middle, 's-Gravenzande and Monster near the coast, Wateringen, De Lier and Maasdijk further inland. The thin dark strip along the north-west edge is the dunes and beach. From above it looks like a city at night, except that the lit-up parts are greenhouses and the people live in the dark gaps.
