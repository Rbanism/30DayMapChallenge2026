# 21Nov_OpenStreetMap: Who mapped Delft?

Every building in Delft on OpenStreetMap, 39,711 in all, coloured by the year it was last edited. Purple means untouched since around 2013, orange means edited recently.

I expected years of work by many different mappers. Instead nearly everything is orange: 89% of Delft's buildings were last edited in 2025 or 2026, and 2026 is by far the most common year. Only a few buildings keep an older timestamp, such as some sheds, a few blocks in the north-west and some buildings on the TU Delft campus.

One mapper, Jvitegem, made the latest edit to 80% of all buildings. Dutch OSM buildings come from the government's BAG register, and a few volunteers keep them in sync with each new BAG release. So a "last edit" map mostly shows the latest maintenance round rather than who first mapped the city.

Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (building metadata via Overpass), municipal boundary from CBS/PDOK.
