# 24Nov_Network: The fietsknooppunten

The Netherlands has a cycling network you navigate by numbers instead of street names. At every knooppunt (junction) a green sign tells you which number comes next. You only remember a list such as 25, 22, 21, 20 and follow the signs.

This map draws that network for the area between Den Haag, Delft and Westland in a transit-map style: teal lines for the links (OSM `route=bicycle` + `network=rcn`) and white circles carrying the junction numbers.

There are 156 numbered junctions and 459 km of links in this 20 x 22 km box. The grid is tighter in the polders of Westland and Midden-Delfland and opens up in the dunes north of Den Haag. Inside the city you can see the links follow park and canal routes rather than main roads. Numbers repeat (there are several 23s and 81s) because they are only unique within a regional network. That works because the next number only has to differ from the one you are standing at.

Grey lines and names are municipal boundaries, for orientation.
