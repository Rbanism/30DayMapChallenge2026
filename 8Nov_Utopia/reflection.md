Because I am graduating on the topic of lunar territory, I chose the Lunar South Pole for this map challenge. 
Through this challenge I hoped to get better acquainted with making maps of its surface. 
I chose a theme (elevation) that is easily and publicly accessible through NASA its website and quickly found the data I needed. 
I have used RStudio before in a course to make maps and got really interested in it. 
Participating in this challenge allowed me to play around with code to visualize and to transform data. 
Since I am new to the R world I was in need of some guidance, so I used AI to tutor me and with troubleshooting. 
It did not write my codes, it did help me fix them. 
I learned to read a raster's summary and to simplify it with the aggregate argument. 
Before I started I had in mind how I wanted the map to look like.
I converted the raster into a table of points (x, y, z(elevation)) to translate it to a nice map. 
My idea was to create a round map, and I decided along the way to project it over a gray toned square.
Then I cut out the circle by keeping the points within a certain radius of the pole. 
Lastly, I was playing around with the visualization tools, which I had also done in the course, and learned some new tricks such as fonts, grid lines, etc. 
The logo placement then was a struggle and I wouldn't have managed without the help of some AI.
