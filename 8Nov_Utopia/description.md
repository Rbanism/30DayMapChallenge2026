This map shows the Lunar South Pole region as mapped by NASA LRO/LOLA mission in 2023. 
The region is mapped from 80°S to the pole itself and the square covers 608 by 608 km, centred on the pole (the white dot).
All NASA's candidate regions for the first crewed Artemis landing all lie within this map. 
In the new space race, all eyes are on the polar regions as they may contain ice water in the crater floors that never see sunlight. 
The map shows the craters, with their elevation relative to the Moon its mean radius. 
The darker the lower the terrain. 
Within this map the terrain ranges from about -7300 to +7000 m in height. 
The grid and rings mark distances from the pole. 
The data comes directly from NASA: NASA LRO/LOLA south polar elevation model, 80 m/pixel (Barker et al., 2023, *Planet. Sci. J.* 4, 183), simplified to 640 m/pixel for the benefit of the map making process.
